Purchasers of [DEX 3 via the Microsoft Store](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/p/dex-3/9mz54hzxs1h6?activetab=pivot:overviewtab) will be treated with a secure and simple checkout system that allows them to painlessly download and install.
